# 4. Show the answer only once its marks are ready

**Status:** accepted

## Context

Scoring needs the re-asked answers and their verdicts, about 2 seconds after the answer itself. The answer could be shown at once and marked afterwards.

## Decision

Hold the answer until it is scored. Meanwhile, show the three steps as they happen.

## Why

- A first reading anchors what follows. A student who has read an unmarked answer has already started to accept it.
- Marks that appear on text already on screen are an unannounced change, the kind people often fail to notice.
- A wait of about 4 seconds is past the point where people need to see that work is in progress, so each step is named as it starts.

## Consequences

- The student waits about 2 seconds longer for the first text.
- The progress steps also teach what the marks are based on: passages, then an answer, then re-asking.
