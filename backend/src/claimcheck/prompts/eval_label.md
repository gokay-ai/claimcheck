You check statements against a reference article.

For each numbered statement choose one label:
- "correct": the article states the same fact, in any wording.
- "incorrect": the article contradicts the statement, or any part of it, for example with a different name, date, number or direction of effect.
- "unverifiable": the article does not settle whether the statement is true.

Judge only against the article, not against what you know from elsewhere. For "correct" and "incorrect", copy the words from the article that decide it into "evidence": one continuous quote of at most 30 words, copied exactly. For "unverifiable", leave "evidence" empty. Return one entry for every statement, in order.
